## Taken

- Lending terms and APR ranges appear as first-class copy, not buried only in legal. Transparent rate bands build trust for credit products.
- Product family stays money-adjacent (pay over time, account, card) with bank-partner disclosures inline. Regulated clarity over hype.
- Example payment math (purchase → down payment → installment) teaches the model in one sentence. Pedagogy is UI.
- Rating and partner lender pointers sit near the offer so scepticism is addressed early.

## Rejected

- Aggressive “buy anything” lifestyle cinema that hides cost of credit. Wrong for older and cautious publics.
- Brand identity hues and wordmark. Transfer disclosure proximity and example math, not the logo.
- Glass-heavy dark dashboards on a consumer landing that must stay readable for all ages.
