## Taken

- Create asks whether you are in the draw or only organizing. Then the name, the date, optional mailing addresses, a budget, a note, and a picture. The invite is a link that can be copied into a text or a social app.
- The draw stays hidden. An organizer or an assistant can reveal one name, or every name. Showing every name also shows who drew the organizer. People who have not responded by the draw are left out. A late join can be added without redrawing the rest.
- Undo is a separate action. Help says the previous matches are deleted, and that redrawing after an undo requires emails even if the first draw did not.
- A draw-now mode can start from names alone. Help says those people then cannot see their own result. The organizer holds the reveal. The tradeoff is written down.

## Rejected

- The costume voice and the holiday decoration. The hidden result and the explicit undo transfer. The mascot does not, and it is the wrong voice inside a money app.
- Requiring an account before a guest can answer the link. The join article says log in or create an account.
- An undo whose cost (emails required before the next draw) shows up after the button. Say it on the action.

## Not applicable here

- Pix, a receivables list, and a subscription charge. The budget is a note, not a payment.
- Dark mode. It was not described.
