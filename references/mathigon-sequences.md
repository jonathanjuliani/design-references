## Taken

- The terms are on the row before the change is written in the gap between them.
- The rule is one sentence after the row, naming the operation. It does not arrive before the terms.
- A different rule is a new row. The first row is left as it was.
- The next section stays closed until the current pattern is finished. The hold is the lesson.

## Rejected

- The opening examples about earthquakes and stock prices. The stakes are wrong for a number puzzle.
- A photograph of a skier used as a sequence. That is a different metaphor, and it is their picture.
- "Reveal all steps", which dumps the rest of the answer at once. A skip is not a think hold.
- Their specific sequences. The pattern is the gap and the sentence, not those numbers.

## Not applicable here

- A course path, an account, and a button that skips ahead. The film has a timeline, not a lesson player.
