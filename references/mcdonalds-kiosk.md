## Taken

- Accessibility is part of the kiosk itself: a reach mode that brings controls down, a screen magnifier, audio navigation, and a call button that alerts staff.
- The payment prompt names every way to pay ("insert, tap or swipe") and keeps Cancel available until payment is sent.
- The confirmation screen shows the order number and returns to the start on its own after about ten seconds, with an exit button for anyone faster.
- Minus is disabled at a quantity of one, so a stepper cannot reach zero by accident.
- The walkthrough report is https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6596e61f30a4ae80fc22b2a0/65b3303b9de42408a44e224a_Chettiar-Report-McDonaldsKiosk.pdf.

## Rejected

- The cart behind a button. Checking the total costs a tap, which is where trust slips.
- Carousels and option lists with no visible sign they scroll. Public UX critiques of this kiosk name it as the main failure, with older users missing options entirely.
- Small, light-weight grey text, named in the same critiques.

## Not applicable here

- The headphone jack and physical navigation pad are hardware, not something a web kiosk can ship.
