## Taken

- The hero is one shader field. The sentence and a single action sit on it. The graphic is the background of the claim.
- Motion is described as events the scene can answer: appear, scroll, hover, pointer. That is a vocabulary, not a reel of one-off tricks.
- Later blocks stay live scenes, and where the result embeds is its own section.

## Rejected

- The particular flare and the field's color. Identity.
- A grid of effects you cannot take without an account. Gating the examples hides what the page is selling.
- A shader backdrop used as a substitute for an interface.

## Not applicable here

- Selling a graphics tool, where the field is the product. Wrong behind forms, tables, or any surface that has to stay still to be read.
