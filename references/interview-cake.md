## Taken

- The lesson states the failure it is built to prevent: looking up the finished answer and still not knowing how to get there.
- When you are stuck, the next breakdown step is the unit of help. The answer is the last step, not the first.
- A full sample question sits on the public page, so the method is visible before an account. The prompt, the constraint, and the wrong first approach are all in prose.
- One language choice is meant to apply across the site, including reference material. The lesson does not fork into a separate page per language.
- Reference concepts (complexity, search, data structures) are a library beside the questions, not a second course with a different voice.

## Rejected

- The specific stock-price puzzle and its solution. That is their content, not a pattern.
- A single authorial persona as the brand. The craft is the stepped reveal.
- Dumping every breakdown on screen at once. The point of the step is that the rest stays closed until you need it.
