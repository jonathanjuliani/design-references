## Taken

- The platform section is three jobs, shown as the product: step-by-step instruction, an editor in the browser, and output you can check. Help sits in that same frame rather than a new tab.
- Catalog rows carry level and hours (beginner, 24 hours). Choice is a path length, not a feature grid.
- A quiz for "not sure what to learn" is a secondary entry next to the catalog, so the default remains browsing courses.
- The hero animation can be paused. Motion is present and optional.

## Rejected

- Logo, certificate badges, and employer logo walls. Identity and social proof, not the lesson structure.
- Live bootcamp and team-admin pitches as the learner UI. Those are other audiences on the same homepage.

## Not applicable here

- An actual lesson was not started. Pane behavior under error, hint, and success states is still unread.
