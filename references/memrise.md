## Taken

- Reasons to learn are concrete situations (exam, new job, family, travel), and each one points at a practice, not a level number.
- Practice is a list of jobs: pronunciation, verb forms, sentence building, listening, and speaking with feedback. Review is one of those jobs, not the whole app.
- Native-speaker video is the differentiator they state: hear speed, accent, and slang, not a synthetic voice reading a card.
- Exam practice is its own mode (timed conditions, feedback on the grade), separate from phrase drills.
- A short daily duration is the commitment they quote. The unit of study is a session you can finish.

## Rejected

- Persona nicknames and lifestyle photography. Costume for the same practice list.
- Any clip style or face that identifies the product. Identity.

## Not applicable here

- A recall card with a playing clip was described, not used. Image motion is the stated medium, not a watched transition.
