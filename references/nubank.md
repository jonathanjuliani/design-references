## Taken

- A charge is a short sequence inside the Pix area: amount, an optional message, then the code, then share. The message is written before the code exists, and it can be skipped.
- The last step shares a link. The link opens a page that holds the code. The chat app carries the words around the charge. The bank stops at the object.
- Paying ends on a confirmation of the amount and the recipient, before the password. A payment link from this app can open straight onto that confirmation.
- The article separates a random account key from a copia-e-cola code. The code carries the amount. The key does not.
- Appearance is a separate setting: light, dark, or follow the phone. The company announced that in 2023. This read did not render either theme, so state colors were not checked.

## Rejected

- The article illustration and the colored headline. That is the brand surface, and it is not the charge flow.
- Reading this as a book of receivables. The steps are one charge at a time. There is no list of who owes what, and no overdue state.
