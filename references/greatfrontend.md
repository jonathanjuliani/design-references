## Taken

- The page is the bank. Each row carries the task, the format (UI coding, JS functions), difficulty, language or framework, and how many people finished it.
- A recommended starter list is one sentence above the full bank, so a newcomer is not dropped into 500 rows with no entry.
- Premium is a property of a row, not a separate marketing site. The free list still reads as the product.
- "Mark complete" is a row action. Progress is on the item you are scanning, not a distant dashboard.

## Rejected

- Solution code, test harness chrome, and any branded editor theme. Not in this read, and identity anyway.
- Gamified decoration on a question index. The scan is the job.
