## Taken

- Insights is sold as one live dashboard inside banking: charts and tables that update as you slice, not a separate analytics product to export into.
- AI is secondary to the picture: trends, ad-hoc questions, and notifications/recaps sit on top of categorized cash flow—not a chat-only money app.
- Drill-down is the trust move: every trend or alert opens the transactions behind it. Numbers stay auditable.
- Categories are editable by the user. Automation proposes; the owner still shapes the taxonomy.
- Included-in-plan pricing removes a second purchase decision from the UI story.

## Rejected

- Dashboard walls of charts without a primary question. Density without a job fails this audience.
- AI answers without source drill-down. In finance that reads as opaque, not smart.
- Decorative glass layers on every card. Transparency should clarify hierarchy, not decorate empty panels.

## Not applicable here

- Access rules (all-accounts users) are Mercury-specific. The notification-plus-drill-down pattern transfers; the entitlement model does not.
