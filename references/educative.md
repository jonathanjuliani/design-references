## Taken

- The contrast is explicit: mastery is built in the browser, not generated and not watched. Quizzes, challenges, and projects are the course, with no local setup.
- Course rows carry hours and a rating. The catalog is scannable before any testimonial.
- Testimonials describe the same structure (interactive walkthrough, text instead of video, AI that does not hand over the answer). The social proof restates the model.
- Paths are named by job (system design, coding patterns, AI), so the homepage is a map of courses rather than a single hero feature.

## Rejected

- Founder headshots and employer names as the layout. Credibility, not the reading pattern.
- A video-player default borrowed from other course sites. This product's point is that the text is the lesson.

## Not applicable here

- A course widget and its assessment chrome were not opened. Density here is the catalog and the claim, not a measured lesson page.
