## Taken

- AI is staged as an operator that prepares work, not as an autonomous money-mover. Every money action ends in explicit review and approve.
- Conversation shows live account context (balances, cards, vendors) inline with the request. The chat is grounded in the user’s data, not a generic assistant pane.
- Control language is primary: permissions respected, traceable answers, escalate to humans. For finance, control is the feature.
- One job per demo block: categorize, issue card, send payment, set auto-transfer. The pattern is task completers, not a feature grid of equal weight.
- Navigation promise is “no tab thrash”: natural language replaces hunting menus while keeping an approval step.

## Rejected

- Autopilot that executes transfers without a review step. Wrong for regulated money UIs even if the marketing is exciting.
- Brand terminal aesthetic or signature chat chrome. Identity and decoration, not the approval pattern.
- Cinematic ambient motion around the chat. The conversation and staged action are enough motion.

## Not applicable here

- Full Command surface requires an authenticated Mercury account. Marketing demos transfer the approval pattern; they do not prove mobile density or accessibility for older users without a product login check.
