## Taken

- The homepage card is a still. The linked file opens as the editor: a stage, a layer list, and a timeline.
- Design and Animate are two modes of the same file. Time lives in Animate.
- Layers are named by the change they make, and the play control moves a time cursor across them. The picture on the stage changed as the cursor advanced.
- Play becomes pause. The animation can be stopped.

## Rejected

- The specific stills in that file. Identity of the example, not the editor.
- A prompt card sitting over the stage. It hides the only preview.
- A timeline copied into a product that is not an animation tool. A playing reel of interface states is a different pattern, already captured elsewhere.

## Not applicable here

- Editing one motion file. Wrong as decoration on a document that has to stay still.
