## Taken

- Home-base framing: all accounts, one clear view, collaborate with partner/advisor without a second SKU. Shared clarity is the job.
- Jobs are sequential and plain: track, budget, collaborate, plan. Older and newer users can map themselves without jargon.
- Transaction review and subscription detection are concrete control loops—mark reviewed, spot leaks—not abstract “AI insights.”
- Cross-platform (web, iOS, Android) is a first-class claim. Households need the large-screen planning surface.
- Connectivity breadth (many institutions) is trust proof for PFM, stated in numbers.

## Rejected

- Chat-first roast personality as the primary chrome. Monarch’s calm dashboard is the transferable part for multi-age finance.
- Card grids of equal features with icons. Prefer job sections with one outcome each.
- Dark cinematic heroes for a daily money home base. Light, readable hierarchy fits the audience better.

## Not applicable here

- Built-in AI assistant depth is lightly marketed here versus Copilot/Cleo. Re-check in-product AI chrome on a logged-in pass before treating chat patterns as Monarch’s strength.
