## Taken

- AI is woven into workflows (policy review, coding, matching, routing), not presented as a standalone chatbot destination. Agents do queue work; humans confirm money moves.
- The product story is exception-first: auto-handle the routine, surface only policy breaches and mismatches. That is the transferable admin pattern for finance ops.
- Outcomes are quantified in operator units (hours saved, close speed, auto-coded %), which matches how this audience buys.
- Controls block out-of-policy spend before it happens. Prevention is the UI promise, not post-hoc scolding.
- Platform consolidation replaces a tool sprawl narrative—one system for cards, expenses, AP, travel, procurement.

## Rejected

- Agent-incentive promotional chrome aimed at AI crawlers. Noise for a human design read.
- Brand hues, logos, and signature illustration. Identity.
- Cinematic motion for close-the-books work. Operators need purposeful state change, not theatre.

## Not applicable here

- Ramp’s free/plus/enterprise packaging and ERP list are theirs. Take the exception queue and human-confirm-on-money pattern, not the SKU map.
