## Taken

- The kiosk brought a known mobile ordering flow into the store instead of inventing a new one. Familiar patterns beat novel ones for a customer with no one to ask.
- A single, plainly placed upsell before checkout is what raised the order value. One offer at the review step works better than offers spread through the flow.
- Removing the social friction of ordering from a person is named as a goal. The interface should never make the customer feel watched or rushed.

## Rejected

- Predictive, personalised upsell. It needs an account or order history, and a snack-bar kiosk has neither.
- Merchandising for limited-time items as the main visual. It competes with the order and the total.

## Not applicable here

- The festival pop-up variant and kitchen-display bypass are operations, not customer interface.
