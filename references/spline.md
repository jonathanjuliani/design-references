## Taken

- The first screen is a live 3D scene, and the primary action is a prompt sitting on that scene.
- A floor grid and floating objects make the space readable as a scene rather than a flat picture.
- Examples below are grouped by job (site, product, mockup, character). That is how you browse a 3D tool.
- A second product is described as a flat canvas for interface and motion, kept separate from the 3D editor.
- Shipping is its own later section, after the scene has done the convincing.

## Rejected

- The mascot and the specific floating objects. Identity.
- Objects that only drift. Atmosphere that never explains the tool is decoration.
- A prompt-on-a-scene hero copied into a product that is not generative.

## Not applicable here

- Choosing a 3D tool, where the scene is the proof. Wrong for an operator surface that should be a document on first paint.
