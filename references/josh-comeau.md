## Taken

- Post metadata is one sentence ("filed under a category on a date", plus "last updated") instead of a row of separate badges.
- Each index entry has a title, a subtitle line and a short excerpt. The subtitle does the work a tag list would.
- A "popular content" list beside the latest posts gives a second way in that recency alone doesn't.
- Category links are a small, bounded set, separate from the post list rather than repeated on every row.

## Rejected

- The illustrated, playful identity (sky, clouds, rainbow art). It is the author's signature and the opposite of a quiet archive.
- Centred post headers. Left-aligned headers scan better in a long technical archive.
- Emoji in titles as a recurring device.

## Not applicable here

- Interactive in-post demos are the product here. They are content, not a layout pattern to borrow.
