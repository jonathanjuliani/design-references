## Taken

- The picture waits. The reader starts it, or steps it. It does not loop as atmosphere.
- Cells take their turn in visit order and then stay. The order is the explanation.
- Two methods are two pictures side by side. What differs is which region grows, and both pictures remain.
- The fill stops when the goal is reached. It does not keep moving after the point is made.

## Rejected

- The named moving character and the credited sprite art. That is their cast.
- Game maps, terrain costs, and "the faster search" as a virtue. Speed is the wrong moral for a patient puzzle.
- Code listings. The grid carries the idea; a program does not.
- Treating the shortest path as a race.

## Not applicable here

- A programming implementation guide. The film shows the grid, not how to code it.
- An audience of game developers. The side-by-side step transfers. The theme does not.
