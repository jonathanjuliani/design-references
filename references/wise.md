## Taken

- Hero is the job with a live rate/calculator pattern: see what the recipient gets before signup. Transparency is the product UI.
- Speed and certainty claims are specific (most arrive in seconds; rate lock window). Vague “fast and secure” is avoided.
- Safety section is concrete: regulation, safeguarded funds, fraud checks at volume. Multi-age audiences need plain structure over novelty.
- Support channels are listed as always-on. Accessibility of help is part of trust chrome.

## Rejected

- Dense crypto-style motion or dark glass excess for a mass-market remittance audience.
- Brand green identity and wordmark. Keep the calculator-first idea, not the hue.
- Feature forests that bury the fee and rate. The first question is “what do they get?”

## Not applicable here

- Product availability and interest features vary by region. The transparency calculator pattern transfers; the product matrix does not.
