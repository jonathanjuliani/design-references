## Taken

- Create is one form in a fixed order: amount, an optional specific client, a short description, the due date, then payout speed, payment methods, an early-payment discount, then whether late payment is allowed and at what fine and interest.
- Card starts on. Pix is a switch. The customer is not debited. They open the link and pay.
- Reminders go out on WhatsApp and email on a fixed calendar: when the plan starts, seven days before for a monthly cycle or longer, two days before, the due day, and two days after if late payment was allowed.
- The list has status words. The link can be copied again only while the charge is pending or late. Expired, paid, and cancelled hide the link, and the help says to create a new charge.
- A one-off charge and a recurring plan are two entries under Sell, rather than one form with the mode buried at the end.

## Rejected

- A tone picker. The reminders are a schedule. The sender does not choose a voice.
- Putting instant payout in the customer's path. That setting is the seller's cash timing.
- A payment-link product that cannot take Pix, if the job is a Pix charge. This article points Pix at the charge-management form.

## Not applicable here

- Dark mode. It was not on the page.
- A guest claiming an item, or a draw.
- US subscription trials. The customer here is being invoiced, not reminded of a card on file.
