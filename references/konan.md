## Taken

- Product is one ops surface for policies, models, simulation, and monitoring—not a consumer loan shop. Lending OS clarity.
- Explainability is explicit: every loan decision should be understandable in dashboards. For credit AI, opacity is a reject.
- No-code policy building plus bring-your-own models. Control of risk appetite is a first-class setting, not a hidden config.
- Outcomes are lender metrics (NPL reduction, acquisition, onboarding time). Speaks the buyer’s dashboard language.
- Simulation before deploy. Safe change management is part of the UI story for regulated credit.

## Rejected

- Consumer BNPL playfulness or roast-style AI personality on a credit-risk console.
- Cinematic marketing motion over dense policy tools. Operators need purposeful state, high information density.
- Brand identity. Transfer explainability and simulate-before-deploy, not the Synapse visual system.

## Not applicable here

- Generic SaaS FAQ filler on the page is noise. Ignore support boilerplate; keep the decisioning workflow craft.
