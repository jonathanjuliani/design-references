## Taken

- Light, calm corporate site that explains app advantages with product UI mockups rather than abstract finance metaphors.
- Structure includes company, career, and blog alongside product story—trust for a bank brand includes institutional pages.
- Micro-motion stays purposeful (section reveal, UI kit samples) rather than cinematic WebGL for a mass banking audience.
- UI kit presentation signals systematic components. Consistency reads as safer than one-off art direction.

## Rejected

- Heavy award-theatre cursor and page-transition gimmicks if they slow task reading for older users.
- Brand teal identity as a transferable hue. Keep calm light surfaces and mockup-led proof, not the palette.
- Equal icon feature soup. Prefer mockups and clear service explanations.
