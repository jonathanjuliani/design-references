## Taken

- The article opens with prose under a small name and role line. There is no hero image or title banner.
- Demos sit inside framed blocks with generous space around them, so examples are visually separate from the argument.
- Motion is reserved for things that explain (a demo, a press state). Navigation and lists don't animate.
- A single neutral ground in light, with no tinted panels, keeps the attention on the text and demos.

## Rejected

- The very small body size. It suits short craft notes but tires on a long technical post.
- An announcement bar above the article. It is promotional chrome on a reading page.

## Not applicable here

- Animated demo players are specific to articles about animation.
