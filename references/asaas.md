## Taken

- Reminders cannot be sent without a charge. On the customer, they are checkboxes in three groups: before the due date, overdue, and paid. Each group has a column for the business and a column for the customer. Changes save on their own.
- A sibling help article describes sharing a finished charge as one button with two outcomes: open WhatsApp with the invoice link, or copy the link. It says neither path adds a fee. The message is written in WhatsApp.
- Another sibling article describes create as a wizard: amount and an optional description, payment methods, interest and a fine, optional files, the customer, then a summary that can step back before finish.
- Help names the situation of a charge as waiting for payment, confirmed (paid, not yet available), received, or overdue, plus refund, review, and chargeback. Those are the words. The list that uses them was not on screen.

## Rejected

- A tone control. The channel article lists WhatsApp, email, SMS, an automated voice call, and post for boletos. Timing is configurable. Wording is not offered as a choice.
- Blocking payment of an overdue charge by asking support. That is a back-office request, and it applies to every charge. It is a poor pattern for a single late customer.
- The voice-call robot and the posted boleto. Wrong channel for a WhatsApp-first micro-business.

## Not applicable here

- Dark mode. It was not described. The steps use a side menu, so this read is the web panel.
- A guest RSVP, a draw, or a trial the user typed in by hand.
