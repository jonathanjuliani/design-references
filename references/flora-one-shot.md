## Taken

- The link opens the project, not the marketing homepage. A frame holds the workflow, and the nodes inside it are videos.
- Nodes are named by the change they make: the source clip, then a character replacement, a new background, a weather pass. An edited node also carries the model that made it.
- A play control sits on the video. Edges leave the node toward the next edit.
- The view was zoomed to one node. The other node names were in the page, so the graph is a sequence even when the camera is tight.
- The bar under the canvas is the project: title, author, a one-line description of the workflow, clone, and copy link. Clone is how you leave with the graph.

## Rejected

- The footage itself, and the model name as a logo to repeat. The name of the change transfers. The brand on the node does not.
- A cookie sheet that blurred the canvas until it was dismissed. Do not treat a consent wall as part of the workflow.

## Not applicable here

- Editing the graph. This was a view. Clone was offered. The nodes were not rewired in this read.
