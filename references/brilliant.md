## Taken

- The session is described as visual and interactive: you work a concept until it clicks, then the next problem. Lecture is not the unit.
- A tutor is framed as questions plus visual guidance aimed at the stuck point, not as an answer dispenser.
- Progress is a small set of habits (streaks, levels, daily goals) after the learning claim, not instead of it.
- Subjects are listed as concrete courses (functions, Python, circuits, regression), so the catalog is checkable.

## Rejected

- The tutor's name, character, and any signature diagram style. Identity.
- Award counts and review totals as the design. They sit beside the method; the method is what transfers.
- Assuming the lesson canvas was seen. This read is the homepage pitch.

## Not applicable here

- The interactive problem canvas itself did not open. Motion types are omitted until a lesson is watched.
