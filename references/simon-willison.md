## Taken

- Tags show their post counts. The count helps a reader choose a filter before clicking.
- Search sits at the top of every page, which fits an archive people come back to look things up in.
- Several content types (entries, links, quotes, notes, guides) share one stream with a type label, so one archive serves many formats.
- A second column of highlights gives returning readers the best posts without paging.

## Rejected

- Boxed tag chips at high density across the header. At this volume it reads as noise, the same problem a chip-per-row list has.
- The default-browser visual style. It works for this author's volume and voice; it isn't a polish target.
- Sponsor bar between header and content.

## Not applicable here

- Daily posting volume. A smaller archive doesn't need date-grouped day headers.
