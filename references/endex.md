## Taken

- Agents live where work already is: Excel, Word, PowerPoint, email, Teams—not a separate toy chat that forces export.
- Assignment surfaces (web, mobile, Outlook, MCP) share one review workspace. Hand-off across devices without losing the task thread.
- Traceability of claims back to sources is a product promise. Finance AI that cites is the transferable trust pattern.
- Security certifications listed for regulated firms (SOC2, ISO, GDPR). Enterprise finance needs this near the ask.
- Vertical lanes (PE, IB, real estate, accounting) keep copy specific instead of one generic “productivity” claim.

## Rejected

- Consumer finance roast personality. Wrong for committee-ready deliverables.
- Autopilot that edits client files without a review loop. Same control rule as banking Command.
- Brand identity and logo walls. Transfer in-file agents and citation, not the mark.

## Not applicable here

- Office-suite embedding is Microsoft-centric. The assign–review–trace pattern transfers to other stacks; the Outlook hook does not.
