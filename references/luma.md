## Taken

- The page leads with the event name, then the date as a small calendar chip beside the weekday and the time, then the place. Registration is one card with one primary button. The host and the guest count sit to the side on a wide screen.
- Register opens a short sheet. Two fields keep their labels (name, email). The text inside the fields is an example. One submit button, and a close control. Help says no account is required. A code arrives by email. A guest who is already signed in is prefilled.
- Registration stops by a capacity, a sales end, or an explicit close. Help says past pages stay on the web, so last year's page has to be closed or people register on the wrong event.
- Default reminders are email, one day and one hour before an in-person event. There is no OS permission step in this flow.
- One theme was on screen: a dark field, a light title, a single filled action. A light theme was not shown.

## Rejected

- The example event still offers Register under a line that it ended hundreds of days ago. A finished event should replace that button.
- The cover image and the host's social marks. Decoration and identity, not the RSVP.
- Sign-in in the corner as a second path. The guest job is the one button.

## Not applicable here

- Claiming a dish or splitting a cost. This page is register or not.
- Pix. It is not on the page.
- Using this dark field as the theme of a money app. Only one theme loaded, and it belongs to this example.
