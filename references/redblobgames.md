## Taken

- Color marks only the concept this step teaches. Coloring every role at once was called a mistake, with the fix being to hide all but the current concept.
- Two still drawings can teach a comparison better than a control that moves the picture.
- The first question is what the reader is learning. Motion that does not serve that is left out.
- A sentence can carry a small picture of the thing it names, so the words and the diagram share a mark.

## Rejected

- The author's mascot mark.
- Game maps as the subject. The craft is the one-concept mark, not the maps.
- Recoloring source code to match a diagram. That is a developer-tutorial device.

## Not applicable here

- Hover as the way a reader points at a part. In a narration, the spoken word does that job.
