## Taken

- The page is the app, not a pitch. A breadcrumb says where it sits, and the product rail stays a strip of icons.
- Finished takes are the large column. Two stills stack, and each one carries edit, upscale, and download.
- Under the stack, the batch actions are reuse inputs, retry, and download all.
- The side panel is the job: what the app does, share, an API entry, how many runs exist, one image input, a preview of that input, and generate. Generate states a cost.
- A rail of other examples sits past the panel, so the next app is a thumbnail, not another homepage.
