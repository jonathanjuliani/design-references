## Taken

- Recurring is one tab with two views. Upcoming is a short calendar of the next two weeks. All is the full list. Items with no charge in the past month sit in an Inactive group at the bottom, so they do not look current.
- Add is a plus, then a name from a list of known services or a custom name, then a search that attaches real transactions, then the next date and the amount, then Save at the top. The row is tied to a charge that already happened.
- Cancel is on the row: a menu, then either "cancel this for me" or "view instructions". If the app cannot cancel that merchant, it says so and shows the steps, including when the charge is billed through a platform store.
- Upcoming, and the cancellation assistant, are marked as paid. The full list is the view that is not behind that line. The paywall sits on looking forward and on being cancelled for, not on seeing what you already pay.

## Rejected

- Requiring a connected bank before a list can exist. A trial someone types in should not wait for that permission. Manual add is the escape hatch, and help says it is mobile-only.
- A concierge as the only cancel. The instructions option is the part that transfers: the person still has a way to do it themselves.
- A paid texting assistant. That is a different product.

## Not applicable here

- Dark mode. It was not in these articles.
- An OS permission primer. The gate here is connecting an account, described in text, not a system dialog that was shown.
