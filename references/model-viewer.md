## Taken

- The accessible name and the loaded model are the same object. The label is not a caption beside a different picture.
- Orbiting the camera shows another side of that same mesh. Front and side are one object, not two renders.
- A still poster can stand in if the model has not loaded. The document around the hero (how to start, where the docs are) does not wait on the mesh.
- Turning the object is the inspection. The rest of the page stays a document.

## Rejected

- The particular scanned object, its visor, and its patches. Identity.
- A showcase copied into a product that is not presenting a physical thing.
- The PlayCanvas homepage vehicle example, which is a film, and a viewer where the filename matched the mesh but a drag did not change the picture. A label that the view does not follow is not this pattern.

## Not applicable here

- Embedding one inspectable object. Wrong as atmosphere on a page that is not about that object.
