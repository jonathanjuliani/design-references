## Taken

- The library and the question bank are one system. A question is not an island. It links back to the article that justifies it.
- Audiences are split on purpose: students, clinicians, and educators. The same knowledge base, different jobs (learn, look up, assign).
- Trust is external and specific: a named comparison study, evidence-based clinical reference, not a generic "trusted by doctors".
- An AI mode is framed as turning a question or an upload into an explanation and a Qbank session inside the library. Generation stays attached to the source, at least in the pitch.

## Rejected

- Leaderboard language against other AI products as the visual. The link from question to article is the pattern.
- Any clinical color system or wordmark. Identity.

## Not applicable here

- The article reader and the question runner were not opened. This file is the relationship between them, as stated on the homepage.
