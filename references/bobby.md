## Taken

- Add opens a searchable list of known services, split into popular and all. Each row is one service. Create custom is a row at the bottom of that list.
- The custom form leads with name, description, first bill, and cycle. Reminder, duration, and currency sit lower. Further fields are behind a disclosure. The reminder control defaults to off, so the notification is an opt-in.
- Home is one scrolling list. The amount sits on the row. The store description says the point of the screen is the total of those fixed costs.
- Nothing in the screenshots asks for a bank. The list is what the person typed.

## Rejected

- A different color and logo on every row as the way to scan. That is each service's identity, and color alone fails in sunlight and for color vision. Keep the name and the amount.
- Shipping the tracker on one phone only. The store says iPhone, and not the desktop. A list for Brazil and the US needs both major phones.
- Guessing what the in-app purchase gates. The listing says the app is free with purchases. The paywall screen was not among the shots.

## Not applicable here

- A cancel link and a detected trial. This stores what you type and can notify you.
- Dark mode. The shots on the listing are light. A dark theme was not shown.
- Pix.
