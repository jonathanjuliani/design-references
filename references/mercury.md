## Taken

- Banking products are listed as jobs people already do with money (accounts, cards, payments, accounting), not as abstract pillars. A sceptical founder can match themselves in one pass.
- Trust and coverage claims sit near the CTA: partner-bank FDIC language, sweep limits, and security controls are treated as product surface, not footer fine print.
- Product proof is operational: virtual cards in a few clicks, AI bill fill, receipt attach, universal search. The interface is the argument.
- Automations and AI are framed as busywork removal with human approval still implied in spend controls and permissions—not as a chatbot replacing the bank.
- Type and density stay calm: few competing actions above the fold; quantified social proof (customers, volume, store rating) after the product story.

## Rejected

- Wordmarks, brand hues, and any signature illustration style. Identity.
- Treating “radically different” as the transferable craft. The craft is clarity and proof, not the slogan.
- Dense award-theatre scroll for a regulated money product. Motion should stay purposeful for this audience.

## Not applicable here

- Mercury is business banking for startups. Consumer lending or DeFi surfaces need a different trust and density dial even when the restraint pattern transfers.
