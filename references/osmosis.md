## Taken

- Three tools are one stack: a short illustrated video for the concept, quizzes for retrieval, and spaced-repetition flashcards so the quiz is not a one-time event.
- The video is positioned against the long lecture and the long note. The unit is minutes, with a story order (definition, progression, context).
- A study schedule and high-yield playlists decide what to open next. Priority is a product surface, not a bookmark.
- Flashcards are described as explanations, not bare terms. Recall still carries the mechanism.

## Rejected

- Clinical illustration style and any character in the videos. Identity.
- An AI chat layered on top as the primary study UI. The page introduces a companion, but the stack above is the pattern.

## Not applicable here

- The player, the card session, and the quiz runner are gated. Image motion refers to illustrated video as the medium, which was described on the homepage and the flashcards feature page, not watched frame by frame. Do not add a second file for the flashcards URL.
