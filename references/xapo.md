## Taken

- Dual-rail clarity: USD protected bank balance versus Bitcoin services, with deposit-guarantee language separated from crypto risk. Mixed-asset products need explicit rails.
- Liquidity without selling holdings (BTC-backed borrow) is a clear job statement for this audience.
- Security is layered and concrete (vaults, MPC, access procedures)—not a vague shield icon.
- Rates and offer windows are dated and conditioned. Fast-moving yield claims need verify_after discipline.
- Membership and relationship-manager framing fits high-touch wealth, not mass neobank chat.

## Rejected

- Award-level cinematic scroll if it overwhelms rate and risk reading. Prefer purposeful motion for money pages; re-check live intensity.
- Brand Bitcoin theatre and wordmark. Identity.
- Collapsing fiat insurance language into crypto security language. Mixing those confuses older and cautious users.

## Not applicable here

- Gibraltar membership model and fee structure are product-specific. Transfer rail-separation and disclosure discipline, not the membership SKU.
