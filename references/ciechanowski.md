## Taken

- One control changes one property of the source. The shadow answers that change. The rest of the scene holds.
- A simplified drawing is introduced as a model, and a reading that model would encourage is corrected before the passage moves on.
- A machine is explained one named stroke at a time, with the rest of the machine staying in frame.
- Looking around the model is a separate action from the property change. It is not part of the same sentence.

## Rejected

- The specific room, the projection widget, and the engine as a scene. Those are this author's sets.
- Turning the whole model while the property changes. The look-around earns its own moment or it is left out.
- The article's palette and the particular objects used to stand in for a light.

## Not applicable here

- A page of sliders the reader drags. A narrated piece has the sentence as the control, not a widget.
