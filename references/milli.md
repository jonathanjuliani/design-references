## Taken

- The makers say children tap everywhere, so the story was built so animations do not overlap and do not repeat.
- The note is the read. The app was not opened.

## Rejected

- The animal character and the picture-book look.
- The materials the pictures were made from. Those are the project's signature, not a method to reuse.

## Not applicable here

- Tap-driven scenes. The same limit applies to a narration: one change for the idea being said, and no replay for its own sake.
