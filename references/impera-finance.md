## Taken

- Services are a numbered capital menu (bridging, development, exit, investment). IA does the sales work without card clutter.
- Case studies lead with location, asset class, strategy, GDV, and loan amount. Specific deal math is the proof.
- Process is a short linear path (application → terms → diligence → approval → legal). Complex lending feels navigable.
- Testimonials name roles and firms. Professional lending audiences trust peers over abstract logos.
- Tone is advisory and calm: time-is-finite positioning without startup chrome noise.

## Rejected

- Neobank playful motion or crypto visual language for institutional real-estate finance.
- Equal icon feature grids. Capital products need ordered menus and deal tables.
- Brand identity flourishes. Keep structure and case-study specificity.

## Not applicable here

- Forms and HubSpot flows are lead-gen specific. The numbered product IA and deal-proof pattern transfer to other lending advisories; the CRM wiring does not.
