## Taken

- Protocol jobs are named plainly: supply, borrow, swap, GHO, stake. DeFi marketing that reads like a product map beats metaphor.
- Non-custodial constraint is stated for agents/MCP: prepare unsigned txs, never hold keys. AI integrations must inherit wallet consent.
- Security, audits, and docs are first-class destinations. Trust for on-chain lending is linked, not implied.
- Separate app (wallet-connected) from public marketing/docs. Surface honesty about what needs a wallet.

- Dense WebGL hero as the main idea of a lending market UI. Markets need rates and risk more than atmosphere.
