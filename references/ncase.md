## Taken

- Each entry is a title plus a short line that says what kind of thing it is: a guide, a game, a picture book, a story.
- The index has no motion. The label does the work before anyone enters.

## Rejected

- Emoji used as the cast of a model.
- Character-led pieces. The label is the finding, not the cast.

## Not applicable here

- A catalog the visitor clicks into. In a narration the same job is one spoken line that says the drawing is a model before it starts to change.
