## Taken

- The page is the gradient. The document does not scroll. A preset name and a pair of arrows are how you change it.
- One sentence says what the tool is for, then one action to try it.
- Grain on the field keeps it from reading as a flat color wash. The motion is the material.

## Rejected

- The specific halo and its preset name. Identity.
- Build markers left in the page text (scroll-progress notes, timeline names). Those are residue, not interface.
- A full-viewport shader with no pause. Atmosphere you cannot stop is a debt if the pattern moves onto a task surface.

## Not applicable here

- A brand moment where the field is the whole product. Wrong on a page that has to be read as a document.
