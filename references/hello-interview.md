## Taken

- The lesson names the interviewer's rubric before the content: problem navigation, solution design, technical excellence, communication. The reader knows what "done" means.
- Failure modes are specific (solving the trivial part, spaghetti structure, memorized answers) instead of "be more confident".
- Reading order is explicit, and practice is called out as a separate job from consuming the guide. Quizzes and worked problems sit inside the same course, not on another site.
- Level expectations (mid, senior, staff) change depth, not the outline. One path, different bar.
- Adjacent interview types (low-level design, ML design, frontend) are routed out with a link, so this guide stays one job.

## Rejected

- Company-name dropping as credibility. The craft is the rubric and the sequence.
- Pasting a full architecture as the lesson. The page argues for a delivery order, not a single diagram to memorize.
- Cinematic scroll on a study document. Density here is prose and structure.

## Not applicable here

- Guided practice feedback and diagram interaction were described, not exercised in this read. Do not treat this file as a canvas or simulator spec.
