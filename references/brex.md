## Taken

- Stack is staged by growth stage (startup / mid-size / enterprise) so buyers self-select before feature depth. Audience routing beats a single undifferentiated hero.
- AI is attached to concrete jobs: expense assistant, invoice entry, approvals, accounting suggestions—not a floating general chat.
- Controls-before-spend is central: budgets and limits auto-enforced, tracked in real time. Prevention language matches finance buyers.
- Proof mixes hours saved, automation %, and yield with liquidity. Numbers stay in operator currency.
- Global coverage is stated early (countries) without turning the first viewport into a map collage.

## Rejected

- Brand orange, wordmark, and signature card photography. Identity.
- Blog-led homepage density that competes with the product story. Insights belong after the product claim.
- Heavy glass stacks on dark marketing for older users if contrast is not verified. Prefer calm surfaces over frosted decoration.

## Not applicable here

- Brex’s credit-limit and yield figures are commercial claims, not layout craft. Transfer the stage routing and AI-on-job pattern only.
