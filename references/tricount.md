## Taken

- The group is the object. Add is a plus on that group. The split defaults to equal, and the word for "equally" is the control that opens an exact share or an exact amount.
- Balances suggest who pays whom, in order to cut the number of transfers. A suggestion can be marked paid on that screen. A repayment is entered as a transfer, from the same plus, rather than as another expense.
- Each person identifies themselves once. That person becomes the default payer, and the total at the top of the list is theirs.
- The group has one settlement currency. A line can be typed in another currency, and the help says the daily rate is shown.

## Rejected

- Offering new users a credit card so they can skip typing expenses. That is a second product on the split.
- A paid tier that the same help says was removed, including export and a personal mode, while unequal splits are described as still available and also "planned". The free path should be the one the article can actually complete.
- A suggestion to pay someone who never paid for you, with the reason only in the FAQ. The summary itself has to say why that person is the one to pay.

## Not applicable here

- Pix. The settlement example is in euros.
- A guest page with no install. This article assumes the app.
- Dark mode. It was not described.
