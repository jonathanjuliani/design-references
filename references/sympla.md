## Taken

- The dashboard sits beside the event list. The first control is the time range. Each panel has a control that explains what the number means.
- The sales total is described as gross, and the help says the service fee and the processing fee are inside it. A money figure should say whether fees are in it.
- Channel is a filter: online, added by hand, or in person. The same panels can be read as ticket count or as money. Device is split into computer and phone.

## Rejected

- Hue as the only way to tell channels apart. The article assigns a different color to online, manual, and in-person sales. A charge list needs the word as well.
- A wall of rankings, peak hour, and a twelve-month comparison as the home of a small party. That density fits a producer with many ticketed events.

## Not applicable here

- A guest RSVP with no account, an item claim, a draw, and Pix between friends. This read is the organizer's sales panel. The public ticket page was not opened.
- Dark mode. It was not described.
