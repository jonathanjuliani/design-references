## Taken

- Product UI is the hero: spending line, budgets with rollover, cash flow, subscriptions, net worth—screens do the selling.
- AI learns tagging over time; the claim is quieter automation that improves with use, not a loud chat takeover.
- Hierarchy is glanceable: under/over budget and pending items start the day. Calm density for daily money.
- Craft awards and editor’s choice are secondary proof after the interface. Quality is shown, then endorsed.
- Pricing is honest and ad-free framed. Premium PFM can lead with clarity of cost.

## Rejected

- Platform lock as a universal pattern. Apple-first distribution does not transfer to multi-age household web needs (prefer Monarch when that is the audience).
- Brand identity and marketing illustration stickers. Keep UI-as-proof and quiet AI tagging.
- Cinematic scroll that hides the spending line. The line is the product.

## Not applicable here

- Web availability is marketed; historically Apple-heavy. Confirm current web parity before using as the only multi-platform reference.
