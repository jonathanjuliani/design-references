## Taken

- One lesson has a sequence: visual story, then a review of the symbols from that story, then an end-of-lesson quiz. Recall uses the same pictures as the explanation.
- A question bank and custom tests sit after the lesson quiz, for exam scope rather than for the last video only.
- Patient cases are a later mode (talk through an encounter, get attending-style questions). Didactic memory and clinical reasoning are different screens in one program.
- Plans are split by job (full library and cases, versus cases only), so the product admits you might not need every mode.

## Rejected

- The specific sketch style, characters, and symbols. Identity. Reuse "memory image, then quiz", never their drawings.
- Pricing tables and financing widgets as study UI.

## Not applicable here

- A lesson and a case were not played. Image motion is the visual lesson as the medium.
