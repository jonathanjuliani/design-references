## Taken

- Jobs stay short and mobile-native: get paid, send, save, borrow bridge, card alerts. One idea per section.
- Real-time alerts and security lock language sit next to send/spend. Confidence for frequent small payments.
- Personalization (card designs, tags, stickers) is optional delight after the money job is clear—not the first claim.
- Trust numbers (scams prevented, FDIC subject to terms, active accounts) appear after the product map.

## Rejected

- Whimsy-first chrome (tags, stickers) as the primary pattern for a multi-age professional lending product. Fun is audience-specific.
- Brand green identity and wordmark. Keep job brevity, not the Cash App look.
- Dark glass stacks that fight large-type readability for older users.

## Not applicable here

- Afterpay and stock/bitcoin investing bundles are ecosystem-specific. Transfer alert-and-send clarity, not the full product mix.
