## Taken

- The homepage is one figure on an empty field. There is no document to scan.
- Chrome for discover, projects, audio, and the showreel stays hidden until needed. The standing instruction is to click and hold.
- A showreel and a project index are modes you open and close, not sections stacked down the page.

## Rejected

- The illustrated figure and what it is holding. Signature character, not a convention.
- A short click that does not change the figure. If the verb is hold, a tap that does nothing reads as a dead page.
- Sound as a default. Audio is a control, not the entrance.

## Not applicable here

- Studio presence, where one object is the whole first impression. Wrong for a task surface that has to be scanned.
