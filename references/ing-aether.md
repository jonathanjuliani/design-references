## Taken

- Conversational bar is always present and contextual to the current screen—assistance without forcing a separate bot tab.
- Information architecture collapsed from many product tabs toward mental-model tabs (home / explore / settings) with AI always available.
- Automation is balanced with user control: tone, data access, and how much to trust generated outcomes are user-set.
- Widgets and skills can be generated and saved; generative UI is opt-in structure, not random layout churn.
- Privacy architecture called out as a separate concern for banking AI. Trust is structural, not copy-only.

## Rejected

- Treating a concept case as a shipped bank product. It is a pattern lab, not production chrome to clone.
- Fully autonomous agents that move money without staged approval. The case itself emphasizes control; keep that limit.
- Brand ING identity. Transfer conversational-bar-in-context, not the bank’s visual system.
