## Taken

- Category filters are plain text tabs on one line, with search on the same row. The active tab is marked by contrast, not by a filled pill.
- Every card puts author and date in the same place, in a quieter style than the title.
- The changelog is a separate strip with its own rhythm (dot, title, one line, date), so short release notes don't dilute long stories.
- The dark theme is built from role tokens. Surfaces step up in small increments rather than using borders everywhere.

## Rejected

- The three-column image-card grid. Each card needs commissioned art, which a solo author can't sustain.
- Product-marketing tone in card summaries. It suits a company hub, not a personal archive.

## Not applicable here

- Customer stories and press as categories. They are company-specific content types.
