## Taken

- One serif family carries display, headings and long prose. The page reads as writing first, with a sans only for small metadata.
- The home page opens with a single sentence about who writes and what, then goes straight into the index. No hero image.
- The post header is a stack: kind eyebrow, title, a one-sentence standfirst, topics as plain text links, and dates set aside to one edge. Each piece has one job.
- Content types (essays, notes) are separate lists on the same index, each with a one-line description of what that type is.
- Light and dark keep the same paper-and-ink logic. Dark is a role swap, not an inversion of the illustrations.

## Rejected

- Illustration-led essay covers and the garden metaphor. Both are this site's identity.
- Drop caps on the first paragraph. They read as ornament on a technical post a reader arrived at mid-problem.
- Card grids for essays with empty image slots while art loads. A text row degrades better.

## Not applicable here

- Growth-stage labels on notes (seedling, evergreen). They fit a garden of drafts, not a dated archive of finished posts.
