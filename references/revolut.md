## Taken

- Subscriptions sit under Payments, behind a calendar icon. A charge the app did not file there can be marked from the transaction, with a toggle.
- Stopping the money and canceling the service are two different acts. Help says blocking a subscription blocks that merchant at that amount, blocking a merchant blocks all of their charges, and neither cancels the service. The person is told to contact the merchant.
- Unblock is on the blocked list and on the transaction.

## Rejected

- Using block as cancel. The help exists because those get confused. A trial reminder should open the merchant's cancel, and say that stopping the card is a different act.
- The 2020 post that announces light, dark, or follow-the-phone. It is not the subscription screen, and the two themes were not compared.

## Not applicable here

- A trial that has not charged yet. This flow starts from a transaction.
- Pix, and a fast add that is not "mark this payment".
- The marketing page about subscriptions. It was not used.
