## Taken

- Create starts with a name, then a poster, then the date, the place, and a description. Cohosts, payment details, a capacity, and questions sit in Settings, under the theme controls. Save Draft is what unlocks inviting. Copy Link does not require anyone's phone number.
- RSVP is a response first. Going asks for a name and a phone, then a texted code. Maybe is how a guest sees the details before committing. Help says there is no account and no app.
- A host message is free text, aimed at a guest status, with a cap of ten blasts per event. On mobile the action sits at the bottom of the event. It is a composer with an audience filter, not a menu of tones.
- Cancel is a named action on the event, and guests are told.

## Rejected

- Theme, effects, a font on the title, and a poster picker. That is the party identity. The paid tier also sells light and dark as a personal theme, and help says that tier is US-only. A money app needs both themes as a setting, not as a purchase.
- Collecting money through the US payment apps named in the "why use this" article. There is no Pix.
- Contact sync that texts the invite for you. A group that already exists only needs the copied link.
- Requiring a phone number before the details are visible, which is what Going does. Maybe is the escape. For a shared churrasco link, the details should be on the page before any number.

## Not applicable here

- Claiming a dish, and a draw. These articles do not describe either.
- A receivables list or a trial charge.
