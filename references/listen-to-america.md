## Taken

- The live page says the experience is best with audio, then offers topics and places. It is a chooser.
- The makers describe one portrait drawn as a line while that person's voice plays. That stroke was not watched on this read.
- They also describe a fast scroll that layers many voices. That is the behavior to leave out.

## Rejected

- Many voices at once.
- The portraits, and the election framing around them.
- The continuous line as a metaphor for a journey. The transferable part is one stroke during one voice, then a hold.

## Not applicable here

- A scroll that starts the next speaker. In a narration the next stroke waits for the next sentence.
