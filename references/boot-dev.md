## Taken

- The method is ordered: pick one language, follow one path, write code daily, build projects. Video is explicitly demoted.
- Roadmaps are three named jobs (backend, DevOps, data), each with the technologies inside. You choose a track before a course salad.
- The tutor example refuses the answer and asks which operator would subtract. Help is a question, not a patch.
- Free chapters are the trial. After that, unpaid use is described as read-only, so the gate is named.
- Volume stats (lessons, courses, XP) come after the method. Progress is a game-like layer on a linear path.

## Rejected

- Salary tables and Stack Overflow survey figures as the visual system. They support the career claim; they are not the lesson UI.
- Wizard-hat flavor in the tutor sample. Voice costume, not structure.
- XP and levels copied as a theme. The transferable part is visible progress on a fixed path.

## Not applicable here

- A lesson player with the quiz and editor was not opened. Motion of level-ups was not seen, so motion types are omitted.
