## Taken

- The charge starts from Cobrar, then Link de Pagamento. Amount comes first. The methods to accept come next: Pix, in-app balance, card, or a subset. The payer sees only the methods that were left on.
- A name and a description are optional, and they come after the amount.
- Share is named as WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, or anywhere else. The money app ends when the link exists.
- Help states that a personal account is enough. A company registration is not required to generate the link.

## Rejected

- Making Pix depend on the payer having the same app. Help says balance and saved cards need an account there. Pix pays from a bank account.
- A tone composer. The article offers a link, not a choice of voice.

## Not applicable here

- An overdue list, interest, or a reminder schedule. This is a one-shot link.
- Dark mode. It was not in the article.
- A guest list or a split among several people.
