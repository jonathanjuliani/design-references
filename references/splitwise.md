## Taken

- Add expense is a description, an amount, and a sentence you tap: who paid, and how it is split. Equal is the default. Exact amounts, percent, shares, and an adjustment sit behind that word.
- Tapping a name removes that person from an equal split, and the remainder is divided among the people still on the line.
- Settle is a different action from adding a cost. Where the app cannot move money, "record a payment" marks a transfer that happened outside. Fully settled is an explicit zero, with its own line, rather than a blank screen.
- The summary is the net. The help's example clears two groups with one payment and says what was cleared in each.

## Rejected

- Itemized split and receipt scan locked to a paid tier on mobile. Assigning dishes at a party should not require a subscription.
- In-app settlement through the US and European rails named in the payments article. For Brazil, the part that transfers is recording a Pix that already happened.
- A payment whose delete leaves the balancing rows in place. The help says those rows have to be removed by hand. A settle action should undo itself.

## Not applicable here

- Pix copia-e-cola. It is not offered.
- Dark mode. It was not in these articles.
- Opening the group from a link with no account. The getting-started article starts with sign-up.
