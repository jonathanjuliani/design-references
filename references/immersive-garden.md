## Taken

- The first instruction is to scroll, and the document itself does not scroll. The page keeps its own scroll position for the scene.
- Work is an index of one sentence plus a job label (experience, shop, or corporate), not a thumbnail grid. Most of the home index is experiences.
- A showreel is its own labeled media block. Film is one exhibit, not the page.
- Two manifesto moments are a small label over one large centered sentence.
- Navigation is a single menu control.

## Rejected

- Client worlds, relief models, and the wordmark. Signature.
- A loader that reports complete and then stays up on a flat gray field. In this session the scene never handed off, so the wait explained nothing.
- A luxury case-study world copied into a product document. This audience is hiring presence.

## Not applicable here

- A brand or studio page where the scene is the site. Wrong where first paint has to be a readable document.
