## Taken

- The screen is the agent, not the rest of the product. The rail is new session, files, and skills and integrations.
- The first move is a prompt: add, model settings, dictate, generate. The headline asks what to create.
- Under that, filters (all, marketing, and others) and a grid of templates. Each template is a named job in one line, with a recreate action.
- Opening a template plays the finished clip in a sheet over the grid. It does not open the thread that produced it.

## Rejected

- The clips and their subjects. They are examples. Recreate without the steps is a trailer, not the workflow.
- A prompt homepage that only describes an agent. This one at least shows the session rail and the finished piece. It still does not show the conversation beside the artifact.

## Not applicable here

- A node canvas or a drawing surface. Those are other tools in the same product, and they were not this screen.
