## Taken

- Chat is the product entry: ask anytime, get feedback, route to support from the same conversation. Useful pattern for in-app finance help.
- Autopilot and savings goals are framed as set-and-assist, with machine learning on spending—not a blank dashboard that waits for expertise.
- Personality is intentional and consistent. If the audience wants candor, voice is a design property, not an accident.
- Bank linking via a known network (Plaid) is explained in FAQ language. Connection trust is spelled out.

## Rejected

- Roast-first tone for professional, multi-age, or regulated lending products. Transfer the always-available ask path, not the roast.
- Replacing clear balances and controls with chat alone. Older publics still need glanceable status outside the thread.
- Brand mascot identity. Keep conversation-as-entry, not the character.

## Not applicable here

- Credit-building card and Gen-Z voice are audience-specific. A professional lending or banking ops UI should not inherit the roast layer.
