## Taken

- A Pix charge can carry a due date, interest, a fine, and a discount, and the help says it still settles immediately when paid. A boleto paid by barcode waits about one business day.
- The payer can be offered one object that contains both rails: a boleto with the Pix code on it, under the same commercial terms. Or the charge can be the Pix code alone. The payer chooses the rail when both are present.
- Issued codes are tracked in the charge list. The article does not show how an overdue row is drawn.
- Interest and a fine are written on the charge. The help treats that as part of issuing it.

## Rejected

- Modeling Pix on a boleto's compensation delay. The article's point is that the Pix rail does not wait.
- A consumer "charge a friend" flow. This article is a company account.

## Not applicable here

- Dark mode, the wording of a WhatsApp message, and the visual treatment of overdue. None of those are in the article.
- A marketing page for charge management describes groups for issued, received, and still due. That page was not opened, so those labels are not taken from a screen.
